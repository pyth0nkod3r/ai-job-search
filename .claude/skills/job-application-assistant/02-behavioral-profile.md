# Behavioral Profile

> **No formal assessment (PI, DISC, MBTI, StrengthsFinder) recorded.** Everything below
> is **inferred from work history and project pattern**. Treat as a working hypothesis
> that the candidate can revise at any time, not a fixed personality read.

## Strongest Behavioral Traits *[inferred]*

- **Operates across domains without flinching.** Carrier has moved between ultrasonic NDT,
  telecom BTS field engineering, frontend / full-stack software, network infrastructure,
  and an agentic AI product inside an 8-year window. That kind of pivot pattern signals
  low domain-loyalty and high learning velocity.
- **Builds end-to-end.** Eka Legal, Interview Platform, the agentic Next.js app builder at
  Egoras, the GFA client sites — every major project is owned from prompt (or brief) to
  deployment. Suggests the candidate defaults to "I'll do it" rather than "I'll find
  someone to do it", at least at current scale.
- **Carries both engineering and software without choosing.** Frontend, Python/Django, Docker,
  CI/CD, plus field engineering with the UTG, P&ID reading, BTS work. This is a bridge profile,
  not a specialist profile. Strongest when roles say "full-stack with hardware exposure" or
  "software engineer who can talk to the field".
- **Self-funds learning.** Azure DP-900, AI-900, Python for Everybody specialization, ALX
  Ventures program, federal ITF/SUPA registration. No employer paid for the most recent
  cert stack. Suggests discipline and a high internal drive to upskill.
- **Comfortable with async and remote work.** Most software roles to date have been remote,
  often run in parallel with on-site engineering. Implies self-direction, written
  communication, and a tolerance for time-zone friction.
- **Open to risk and change.** Status is "open to offers" with "willing to relocate" — early
  career, but signaling that the candidate is not looking for the safe 9-5 in Port Harcourt
  for the rest of the decade. *Inferred, not stated.*

## How Miracle Works Best *[inferred]*

- Projects with a clear ship target and a real user at the other end (the cube-os app builder
  has both; the BEng thesis had both; the interview platform has both).
- Teams that move quickly and give ownership ("you own this surface area").
- Roles that combine software with some physical / embedded / systems context — the candidate's
  full self isn't being used if the work is pure front-of-screen.
- Async / remote setups with a written culture, given track record.

## What Drains *[inferred]*

- Pure maintenance work with no clear product surface to ship to.
- Roles that require years of single-stack specialism the candidate does not have (e.g.
  "5 years of pure backend on Java Spring in a financial institution" — the candidate is
  full-stack and would have to over-claim to be competitive).
- Long on-site commitments with no remote flexibility, given the track record.
- Roles that gate on Spanish or German at C1+ — the candidate has these at A1 only and
  would either need to upskill fast or be flagged honestly.

## Management Style Preferences *[inferred]*

- Wants to be told the *what* and own the *how* — the build-end-to-end pattern suggests
  low need for task-level supervision.
- Comfortable with feedback on shipped work; less comfortable with process for its own
  sake.

## Behavioral Notes for Drafting

- When the posting says "team player" or "collaborative", ground in real evidence:
  ALX Ventures partnership, GFA team work, Egoras integration with backend dev. Do not
  hand-wave.
- When the posting says "self-starter" or "autonomous", ground in: agentic app builder
  (built end-to-end), Eka Legal / Interview Platform (full-stack ownership), remote
  parallel-role work.
- Avoid: claims of leadership experience the candidate does not have (no team-lead
  titles in the data). If a posting's behavior section requires leadership of others,
  flag and offer a "led a project / owned a surface area" framing as a stretch.
